This is a **sample entry** so you can see the format before writing a real one.
Delete this file whenever you like.

Each entry in this category is one day of reading:

- **Title**: `Day NN — a short hook`
- **Frontmatter**: `date`, `category: ai-book-a-day`, `book`, and `page`
- **Body**: a few honest paragraphs on what the page said and what I think about it

## What the page said

One page's worth of material. Enough to react to, not so much that the daily
habit becomes a burden.

## What I think

The useful part of reading a page a day isn't coverage — it's that it forces a
daily reaction. Writing one paragraph about a single page is easier to keep up
than taking exhaustive notes.
